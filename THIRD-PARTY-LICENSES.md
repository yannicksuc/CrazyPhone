## Pixel Twemoji 9x (chat emoji font)

Bundled under `assets/twemoji/` and `assets/minecraft/font/default.json` - adds pixel-art emoji glyphs to
the default Minecraft font, so real Unicode emoji pasted or typed into chat render as pixel art instead of
the vanilla "tofu" placeholder box.
